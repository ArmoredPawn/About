An ear-training game

Hear it. Find it. Play it.

The game plays a note, a chord or a short tune. Find it on the piano below with your laptop keys, a click or a real keyboard.

Five stages, then an endless encore that speeds up. You have three lives, and answering in a row multiplies your points.

Or practise one drill

No clock and no lives. It gets harder as you get answers right.

Your laptop is the piano

The home row plays the white keys and the row above plays the black keys. Every piano key below shows its letter.

Other ways to play

  • Click or tap the keys on screen.
  • A MIDI keyboard plugged into your laptop. Any octave counts.
  • Headphones help, most of all with chords.
