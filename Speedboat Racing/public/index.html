How to Play Hydro Rush

🕹️ Controls:
  • W / Up Arrow: Throttle forward
  • S / Down Arrow: Reverse / Brake
  • A / D or Left / Right Arrows: Steer rudder
  • Space / Shift: Fire Nitro Boost
🌊 Lots & Lots of Obstacles:
🪨 Sharp Rocks & Reefs
Harsh collision that ricochets and cuts speed. Steer clear!
💣 Naval Mines
Explodes on impact with a massive blast wave, spinning your boat!
🌀 Swirling Whirlpools
Exerts powerful vortex gravity pulling boats into the center.
🪵 Floating Crates & Logs
Smashes into floating timber; slows your momentum slightly.
🚀 Jump Kicker Ramps
Launches your boat airborne! Fly high OVER obstacles and mines!
⚡ Hydro Boost Pads
Step on glowing neon arrows for an instant turbo speed surge!
